How email alerts work

This site is static (no server), so pinning a card only saves it to this browser — it won't email you on its own. To get emailed when a pinned card gets cheap, you need to save it into docs/data/watchlist.json in the repo once: a script runs every 6 hours, checks that file against the latest prices, and emails you through Gmail if something's below your target (or has dropped sharply vs. its own recent average).

  1. Pin cards from Prices & Deals or By Rarity (the ☆ button).
  2. Optionally set a target price below for each pin — leave it blank to just alert on a big drop (15%+ below its 90-day average).
  3. Click Copy watchlist.json, then Edit watchlist.json on GitHub, paste it in, and commit.
  4. One-time setup (only needed the first time): in the repo's Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions, add GMAIL_ADDRESS (a Gmail address) and GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD (a Gmail App Password, not your normal password — requires 2-Step Verification on the account), and optionally NOTIFY_EMAIL if you want alerts sent somewhere other than GMAIL_ADDRESS.

⚠️ On trust: every "Buy" link on this site goes straight to a TCGplayer product page — TCGplayer is the marketplace of record and handles payment/buyer protection itself, this site never touches your money. Still, a price that's dramatically below a card's usual range (flagged with ⚠️ in the price tables) is more often a data glitch, a mis-listed/damaged item, or a bait listing than a real steal — check the listing's condition, seller rating, and shipping cost before buying anything that looks too good to be true.
